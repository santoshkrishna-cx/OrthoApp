## 🎯 What it is, in one line

Asthimitra turns an enquiry into a **doctor-approved care plan**, sends **verified caretakers and escorts**, records **every act of care** (even offline), and keeps the **family continuously informed** — while enforcing the safety and quality promises the service makes.

## 🧭 How it works — the recovery journey

1. **Enquiry & assessment** → free home visit
2. **Doctor care plan** → designed & approved (no care starts unapproved)
3. **Team assigned** → matched, verified, briefed
4. **Care & monitoring** → daily reports, 2-hour repositioning, physio, escorts
5. **Review & transition** → monthly review → assisted independence

## 👥 The three apps

- **👪 Family** — journey, daily reports, care team, escorts, GST billing + in-app payment (EN/TE/HI)
- **🩺 Doctor** — patients & vitals trends, care-plan approval (the supervision gate), reviews, clinical alerts
- **🚑 Escort** — live trip with GPS/ETA, journey checklist, consultation notes, works offline and syncs later

## v1 decisions (locked)

- **Payments:** in-app (UPI / card / net-banking) — included in v1
- **Market:** Hyderabad
- **Languages:** English, Telugu, Hindi
- **Offline:** mandatory for field staff (caretakers/escorts)
