Asthimitra — logo directions

Asthi (అస్థి / अस्थि, “bone”) + mitra (“friend”): your bone-care friend. Three mark concepts, a wordmark, and app-icon treatments — early sketches to react to, not final art.

The wordmark

Two halves, one word: Asthi in a calm ink weight, mitra carrying the warmth of the brand in teal. Set in a humanist serif for dignity and trust.

Three logomark directions

Each mark works in a rounded app tile and reduces cleanly to a favicon.

Concept 1 Recommended

The A-Bone monogram

The letter “A” for Asthi, its crossbar drawn as a bone. Instantly legible as both an initial and an orthopedic mark — the most ownable and scalable.

Concept 2

The Bone-Heart

A heart holding a bone at its centre — care (mitra) around the clinical core (asthi). The warmest, most emotional read; leans consumer.

Concept 3

The Companion

An arm curved protectively around a person — the mitra / friend and the safe-escort idea in one abstract, human form. Softest, most caring.
