What is this
Asthimitra in one line

Asthimitra is the operating system for the whole home-care service — it turns an enquiry into a doctor-approved care plan, sends verified caretakers and escorts, records every act of care, and keeps the family continuously informed.

Why it's needed
Two gaps families can't close alone

Clinical-skill gap at home

Safe transfers, pressure-sore prevention, wound care and spotting complications are trained skills — not things love can substitute for.

Continuity gap, hospital ↔ home

Appointments get missed, discharge instructions get lost, and unaccompanied travel is unsafe for immobile patients.

Today this runs on phone calls, paper and WhatsApp — it doesn't scale, isn't auditable, and gives families no single trustworthy view. Asthimitra fixes that.
